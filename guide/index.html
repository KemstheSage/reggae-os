Start here

  1. Open the emulator and tap its audio-start control. Play its virtual keys.
  2. On the instrument screen, ALGORITHM chooses track T1–T4; PRESETS chooses a sound. The four lower knobs match the four labels on the screen.
  3. Tap a section button (ENV, LFO, FX, SCL, EDIT, SAVE, ARP or SEQ); further separate taps cycle that section’s pages. Avoid rapid double taps when navigating.
  4. Tap HOME to get back to the instrument area. From instrument play, HOME cycles MIXER → CLOCK → instrument. Hold HOME for settings.
  5. MASTER controls output level. SELECT’s job is configurable in Settings → CONTROL; do not assume it always selects a page.

“K1–K4” means the four lower parameter knobs, from left to right. Turn an action knob to select an operation, then use OCT+ to confirm and OCT− to cancel when prompted. Settings use all four knobs to edit the same selected row. Blank knobs are intentionally unused.

Record a first loop: from instrument play, tap REC and play: the first note starts recording. Tap REC to finish the overdub. Select another track and repeat. PLAY stops. On the stopped STEP editor, REC instead arms step entry. Save the complete session through SAVE → PROJECT.

15 new chord types

Tap SCL to reach CHORD GARDEN. K3 chooses the chord, K4 the voicing. Try MAJ6, MIN7 or MIN9 on organ/piano; they are useful starting colours, not substitutes for offbeat articulation. Existing OFF, DIA3, DIA7, MAJ, MIN, DOM7, MAJ7, MIN7, SUS4 and POW remain available.

↑ means the next octave. CLOSE uses the listed pitches. OPEN raises the second tone an octave; INV1/INV2 raise the first one/two tones. +OCT adds a lower root: when already using four tones it drops a tone to stay within four voices. Extended ninth/6/9 shapes keep their colour tones by replacing the upper root. At the ends of the keyboard, out-of-range tones are omitted. AUTO starts with a close chord, then chooses nearby inversions on each track. An octave jump, a patch/project reset, or playing a note with another voicing starts a fresh phrase. AUTO affects new live chords; recorded notes retain their pitches.

DIA3/DIA7 follow ROOT and SCL; fixed shapes follow the note you play after keyboard quantisation/transposition. Choose POLY under EDIT → VOICE to hear all chord notes. MONO/LEGATO/UNISON play only the root; drum kits ignore chord mode. Eight voices are shared across the four tracks. PROJECT saves the chord settings; loading a user sound deliberately keeps the current track’s chord settings.
