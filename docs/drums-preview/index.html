A new acoustic foundation.

Four playable presets using the FM-1 firmware renderer. Eight short CC0 recordings replace the synthesized Roots drum voices. Dusty and LoFi reshape the same recordings; Ambient mixes recordings with synthesis.

Open the emulator →
Tap Switch on, then Load new acoustic drum kits below the instrument. Turn PRESETS through the drum category. Without the pack, these presets use synthesized fallback drums.

Knobs: Tone · Decay · Snap · Drive. The new preview has separate browser saves. Your FM-1 has not been updated.

Original 80 BPM comparison patterns, rendered through the actual C firmware. No commercial music sampled. These raw auditions are not loudness-matched or mastered; numerical tests do not establish musical quality.

Roots acoustic

Acoustic kick, snare, clap, closed/open hats, tom, cross-stick and cowbell. Short one-shots, with open-hat choking.

Hit order: kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, tom/conga, rim, bell.

Dusty HipHop

Lower tuning, tighter decay, stronger attack and controlled drive.

Hit order: kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, tom/conga, rim, bell.

Soft LoFi

Lower and darker, softer attacks, gentler drive and more relaxed decay.

Hit order: kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, tom/conga, rim, bell.

Ambient Perc

Synthesized low drum, conga and bell with softened acoustic tops and reverb.

Hit order: kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, tom/conga, rim, bell.
